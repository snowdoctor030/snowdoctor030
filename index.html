Projects

PROJ-001

Power Supply — The One That Almost Caught Fire

electronics

My very first electronics project — a simple soldered power supply. Simple in theory, a disaster in practice.

“I soldered all three legs of the voltage regulator onto the same copper path. When I powered it on, the LED wouldn't light up. The regulator was heating up so fast I could smell it. Took me hours to realize what I'd done. I learned more about PCB design that day than any textbook could teach me.”
PROJ-002

555 Timer Clock & 4-Way Traffic Light

logic design

A 4-way traffic light system built entirely with logic gates — no microcontroller, pure hardware logic.

“Everyone in class had to simulate it in Proteus. Most gave up. I spent two straight days — barely sleeping — debugging and tweaking until the simulation worked perfectly. I was the only one who got it running. Seeing those LEDs blink in sequence after all that effort? Pure magic.”
